Prashant Subedi

Creator · AI/ML Engineer & Researcher · Nepal

Prashant Subedi is an aspiring AI/ML Engineer and Researcher from Gaindakot, Nepal, currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Technology (CSIT) at Lumbini ICT Campus, Tribhuvan University. He engineers intelligent systems powered by machine learning and deep learning, turning research ideas into real, usable software. He built and maintains NepalDisaster.com as an open civic information service during the August 2026 Nepal floods to deliver fast, accurate, bilingual disaster information to the public.
